# Source material and attribution

Sebastian Fleming Smith developed the research question, source requirements,
validation rules and review workflow for *The AI Lose Lose Race*. Claude Code
assisted with the original implementation; Codex assisted with this public
edition, its documentation and verification.

The fixtures contain dated material from SEC filings and company publications,
plus recorded model extractions and clearly marked synthetic test cases. Their
original source links are retained. They are evidence for reproducing tests, not
fresh checks or investment recommendations.

Private editorial notes and document metadata were replaced or removed from the
public fixtures. Identifiers and proposal line positions were retained so that
the recorded decision relationships remain valid. Data is explicitly a dated
example, not the maintainer's current private research workspace.

No open-source license has been selected for this repository. Public visibility
does not by itself grant a general license to redistribute the code or third-party
source material. Original source material remains attributable to its authors.
